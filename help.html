Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)
How do I deposit funds into my account? ▼
Go to the Deposit page, select your preferred payment method (bKash, Nagad, or Binance), transfer the funds to the provided details, and submit your Transaction ID. Your balance will be credited after confirmation.
How long does it take for a campaign to go live? ▼
Submitted campaigns are reviewed promptly by our automated filters and moderation team. Campaigns usually become active within 30 minutes to 1 hour.
What should I do if my deposit is delayed? ▼
If your deposit is not reflected within 24 hours, contact us directly via Telegram or WhatsApp with a payment screenshot and your Transaction ID for immediate assistance.
